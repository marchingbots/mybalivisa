Indonesia Visa Types.

There are roughly 110 Indonesia visa types across 11 different categories. We organized them to make things simple.

Choose the option closest to what you want to do in Indonesia.
Still not sure? Contact us and we will point you toward the right pathway.

01

Tourist & Holiday Visas

For people visiting Indonesia for travel, leisure, wellness, family holidays, or short stays.

 02

Remote Worker Visas

For people working remotely while living in Indonesia on a longer-term lifestyle or mobility basis.

 03

Investor & Company Setup Visas

For people investing, establishing companies, or structuring long-term business operations in Indonesia.

 04

Retirement & Long Stay Visas

For people planning to live in Indonesia long term for retirement, lifestyle, or extended stays.

 05

Business & Professional Visit Visas

For short-term commercial meetings, professional visits, market research, or business activity without local employment.

 06

Family & Spouse Visas

For people joining spouses, children, parents, dependents, or family members living in Indonesia.

 07

Work & Employment Visas

For foreigners working in Indonesia under an employer, sponsor, or formal employment arrangement.

 08

Creative, Media & Entertainment Visas

For creative projects, media production, performances, events, cultural work, or content-related activity.

 09

Education & Training Visas

For people studying, training, joining courses, or attending recognised education programs in Indonesia.

 10

Social, Cultural & Special Purpose Visas

For social, cultural, religious, humanitarian, government, or specialised non-commercial purposes.
