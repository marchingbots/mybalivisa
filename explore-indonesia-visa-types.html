Indonesia Visa Types.

Start with the broad visa category that matches what you want to do in Indonesia. Each section links to a more focused visa page.

01

Tourist & Holiday Visas

For people visiting Indonesia for travel, leisure, wellness, or short stays.

 02

Remote Worker Visas

For people working remotely while living in Indonesia on a longer-term lifestyle or mobility basis.

 03

Investor & Company Setup Visas

For people investing, establishing companies, or structuring long-term business operations in Indonesia.

 04

Retirement & Long Stay Visas

For people planning to live in Indonesia long term for retirement, lifestyle, or extended stays.

 05

Business & Professional Visit Visas

For short-term commercial activities without local employment.

 06

Family & Spouse Visas

For people joining family members, spouses, or dependents living in Indonesia.

 07

Work & Employment Visas

For foreigners working in Indonesia under sponsorship.

 08

Creative, Media & Entertainment Visas

For people visiting Indonesia for creative projects, media production, performances, events, or cultural activities.

 09

Education & Training Visas

For study and institutional programs.

 10

Social, Cultural & Special Purpose Visas

For people travelling to Indonesia for social, cultural, religious, humanitarian, or other specialised non-commercial purposes.
