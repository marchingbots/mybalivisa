Official immigration sources.

The government websites, visa information, application portals and legal sources we use when reviewing Indonesian immigration matters.

01

Governing authority.

Indonesia's immigration system is administered by the Ministry of Immigration and Corrections (Kementerian Imigrasi dan Pemasyarakatan) .

Within the Ministry, the Directorate General of Immigration (Direktorat Jenderal Imigrasi) is responsible for formulating and implementing immigration policy. Its functions include immigration services, visas and stay permits, border control, immigration enforcement and immigration information systems.

04

Laws and regulations.

JDIH — Jaringan Dokumentasi dan Informasi Hukum is Indonesia's government legal documentation and information network.

These repositories provide access to regulations, decisions and other legal instruments that form part of the immigration framework.

Current legal repository

Ministry of Immigration and Corrections JDIH

The Ministry of Immigration and Corrections legal information database, providing access to regulations, ministerial decisions and other legal instruments relevant to immigration and corrections.

A legal-document repository for instruments issued under the former Ministry of Law and Human Rights structure. It is useful when researching immigration regulations and legal instruments issued before the current ministry structure was established.

05

How we use official sources.

We use official government sources as the starting point when reviewing an immigration matter.

Visa descriptions explain the available immigration pathways. Application portals show the operational process. Regulations and legal instruments provide the underlying legal framework.

Requirements and procedures can change. We therefore check current government information against the applicable legal sources when reviewing a matter.
