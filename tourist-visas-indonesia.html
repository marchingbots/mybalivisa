Tourist & Holiday Visas

Indonesia Tourist Visas.

For people visiting Indonesia for holidays, surf trips, wellness stays, slower travel, and longer lifestyle stays in Bali.

Some people come to Bali for two weeks. Others stay for months. The right visa depends on how long you want to stay and how you plan to use your time in Indonesia.

Your Options.

01

Visa Exemption

For short holidays and quick trips to Indonesia. Suitable for eligible passport holders visiting Bali for tourism.

  • 30-day initial stay
  • No extension
  • Limited countries eligible
02

E-VOA

The most common Bali tourist visa pathway for short holidays, resort stays, and flexible travel plans.

  • 30-day initial stay
  • Extendable once
  • Eligible countries list
03

CI Tourist Visa (formerly B211A)

Designed for longer tourism stays, slower travel, and people wanting more time in Indonesia.

  • 60-day initial stay
  • Extension to 180 days
  • Conversion to KITAS possible.
04

Multiple Entry Tourist Visit

For repeat travellers visiting Indonesia multiple times per year for holidays and lifestyle travel.

  • Multiple entries permitted
  • 1, 2 or 5-year visa available
  • Suitable for frequent visitors
