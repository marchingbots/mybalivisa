Tourist & Holiday Visas

Indonesia Tourist Visas.

For people visiting Indonesia for holidays, surf trips, wellness stays, slower travel, and longer lifestyle stays in Bali.

Some people come to Bali for two weeks. Others stay for months. The right visa depends on your passport, how long you want to stay, and whether you need extensions or multiple entries.

Choosing the right pathway

Start with how long you want to stay.

Tourist visas are not all the same. The right pathway usually depends on your passport, your intended length of stay, whether you need extensions, and whether you plan to leave and re-enter Indonesia.

Best for very short trips

Visa Exemption

A simple option for eligible passport holders taking short holidays or quick trips to Indonesia.

  • 30-day initial stay
  • No extension
  • Limited countries eligible
Best for standard holidays

E-VOA

The most common Bali tourist visa pathway for short holidays, resort stays, and flexible travel plans.

  • 30-day initial stay
  • Extendable once
  • Eligible countries list
Best for longer stays

C1 Tourist Visa (formerly B211A)

Designed for longer tourism stays, slower travel, and people wanting more time in Indonesia.

  • 60-day initial stay
  • Extension to 180 days
  • Conversion to KITAS may be possible
Best for repeat visitors

Multiple Entry Tourist Visit

For repeat travellers visiting Indonesia multiple times per year for holidays, family visits, and lifestyle travel.

  • Multiple entries permitted
  • 1, 2 or 5-year visa available
  • Suitable for frequent visitors

Other option to consider

Not every visit is tourism.

Some people visiting Indonesia for family visits, social activities, cultural stays, or community-based activities may be better suited to a social activity visa pathway instead of a standard tourist visa.

This matters because the visa category should match what you actually plan to do in Indonesia — not just the fact that you are entering as a visitor.
