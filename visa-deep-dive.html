Immigration Law

Immigration law is the rule book.

Indonesian visas are not just forms and fees. They are permissions issued under a legal framework. The visa you choose should match what you actually intend to do in Indonesia.

This matters because the wrong visa can create problems later. A visa may let you enter Indonesia, but that does not mean it lets you work, invest, run a business, earn income, sponsor family, or stay long term.

Our approach is simple: understand your situation first, then work backwards to the visa pathway that properly fits your activities, timing, and documentation.

The core immigration laws and regulations which currently apply are:

02

Visa types

Different visas do different jobs.

The right visa depends on what you want to do in Indonesia, how long you want to stay, and whether your activities are personal, business, investment, employment, or family related.

Visitor visas, business visas, investor visas, work permits, retirement visas, family visas, and stay permits all serve different purposes. The names can sound simple, but the legal use of each visa can be narrow.

We help you identify the correct visa category before documents are prepared. That avoids wasted time, mismatched applications, and advice that sounds easy but creates risk later.

03

Process

The process should be clear.

A proper visa process has stages: eligibility review, document collection, application preparation, submission, government assessment, issuance, and post-approval steps.

We keep the process structured so you know what is happening, what we need from you, and what is outside our control. Government timelines can change, and approval decisions sit with the relevant authority.

Our role is to prepare the application properly, manage the steps carefully, and communicate clearly throughout the process.

04

Requirements

Documents matter.

Strong applications are built on proper documentation. Requirements vary depending on the visa type, sponsor structure, personal circumstances, and intended activity in Indonesia.

Common documents may include passport copies, photographs, proof of funds, sponsor documents, company documents, insurance, address details, family documents, or other supporting material.

We tell you what is required, what format it should be in, and where gaps need to be fixed before the application moves forward.
