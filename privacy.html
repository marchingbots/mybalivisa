Privacy Statement

Your Personal Information.

At myBalivisa, we take privacy, confidentiality, and legal compliance seriously. We collect and process personal information only where necessary to provide visa, immigration, relocation, and related consulting services in Indonesia.

01

Who we are

We are a visa services business.

myBalivisa assists foreign individuals, families, investors, remote workers, and businesses with Indonesian immigration and related administrative matters.

For the purposes of Indonesian privacy law, myBalivisa may act as a Data Controller when determining how and why your personal data is processed.

This Privacy Statement explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect personal data in accordance with applicable Indonesian law, including the PDP Law, the Company Documents Law, and Indonesian record retention obligations.

myBalivisa is a trading name used by PT Kapan Kita Bersama, an Indonesian foreign investment company (PT PMA) established under the laws of the Republic of Indonesia. References in this Privacy Statement to “myBalivisa”, “we”, “our”, or “us” include PT Kapan Kita Bersama and its authorised personnel, contractors, consultants, and service providers involved in the delivery of visa, immigration, relocation, and related consulting services.

02

Information we collect

Visa work needs personal information.

We may collect and process personal data needed to assess, prepare, and support Indonesian visa and immigration matters.

General personal data may include your full name, nationality, passport information, date of birth, contact details, residential address, marital status, immigration status, and travel information.

Where required for immigration or legal compliance purposes, we may also process specific or sensitive personal data, including biometric information, health-related information, financial information, employment or business information, and tax-related information.

Certain categories of data are classified as specific personal data under Indonesian law and receive enhanced protection.

03

How we use personal data

We use data for the work we are asked to do.

We process personal data for legitimate business and legal purposes connected to our services.

This includes preparing and lodging visa applications, immigration compliance and advisory services, client onboarding and identity verification, communication with immigration authorities and third parties, processing invoices and payments, record keeping and compliance obligations, internal administration and risk management, and responding to legal or regulatory requests.

Personal data is processed only for lawful, specific, and transparent purposes in accordance with the PDP Law.

04

Legal basis and consent

We need a lawful reason to process data.

Depending on the circumstances, we process personal data based on consent, service delivery, legal obligations, legitimate interests, vital interests, or regulatory requirements.

Where required by law, we obtain your consent before processing personal data. Consent may be provided electronically, in writing, through signed engagement documents, online forms, messaging platforms, or other recorded communications.

You may withdraw consent at any time, subject to legal and regulatory retention obligations.

05

Disclosure of information

We do not sell personal data.

We may disclose personal data where needed to provide services, comply with law, or manage the business properly.

We may disclose personal data to Indonesian immigration authorities, government departments and regulators, professional advisers and consultants, payment providers and administrative vendors, translation, notarial, or legalisation service providers, technology and cloud storage providers, and other third parties where legally required.

Where third parties process personal data on our behalf, we require them to maintain appropriate confidentiality and security protections.

Your personal data may also be transferred outside Indonesia where necessary for service delivery, communications, cloud storage, or international administration. Where cross-border transfers occur, we take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate levels of data protection consistent with Indonesian law.

06

Data retention

Some records must be kept.

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for visa services, legal compliance, accounting, taxation, risk management, dispute resolution, or regulatory requirements.

Under Indonesian company document laws, certain records may be retained for a minimum of 10 years.

Indonesian privacy law also requires businesses to establish internal retention policies and destroy or erase personal data once retention periods expire, unless continued retention is legally required.

07

Security measures

We protect what we hold.

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and operational safeguards to protect personal data.

These safeguards are designed to protect against unauthorised access, disclosure, loss, misuse, alteration, and destruction.

Measures may include restricted file access, encrypted communications, password-protected systems, secure cloud storage, internal confidentiality obligations, access controls, and document management procedures.

If a data protection breach occurs that materially impacts your personal data, we may notify affected individuals and relevant authorities in accordance with Indonesian law.

08

Your rights

You can ask us about your data.

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights in relation to your personal data.

These rights may include requesting access to your personal data, correcting inaccurate information, withdrawing consent, requesting deletion or destruction of personal data, restricting or objecting to certain processing, and requesting information about how your data is processed.

We may decline requests where permitted or required by law, including for legal, regulatory, taxation, immigration, accounting, or dispute-related reasons.

We may process personal data relating to minors where necessary for dependent visa applications or family immigration services. Where required, consent must be provided by a parent or legal guardian.

09

Cookies and updates

Websites use basic tools.

Our website may use cookies, analytics tools, and related technologies to improve website functionality, performance, and user experience.

You may adjust browser settings to limit or disable cookies, although certain website functions may not operate correctly.

We may update this Privacy Statement from time to time to reflect changes in law, business operations, or compliance requirements. Updated versions will be published on our website.
