Privacy Statement

myBalivisa is the immigration services brand of PT Kapan Kita Bersama. This statement explains how we collect, use, disclose and protect personal data.

Last updated: 3 October 2026

1. Who we are

myBalivisa provides immigration, visa and related support services in Indonesia.

PT Kapan Kita Bersama may act as a Personal Data Controller for personal data processed in connection with these services.

2. Information we collect

The information we collect depends on the service you request. It may include:

  • identity and contact information;
  • passport and travel document information;
  • photographs and identification documents;
  • visa, immigration and stay permit information;
  • family and relationship information;
  • employment and professional information;
  • company, investment and business information;
  • financial and payment information;
  • correspondence and documents you provide to us; and
  • technical information generated when you use our website.

3. How we use your information

We may process personal data to:

  • respond to enquiries;
  • assess immigration or regulatory requirements;
  • provide and manage our services;
  • prepare and process applications;
  • verify information and documents;
  • communicate with relevant government authorities and service providers;
  • process payments and maintain business records;
  • protect our systems and prevent misuse; and
  • comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

Where applicable law requires consent, we will request that consent before processing the relevant personal data.

4. Government authorities

Immigration and regulatory services can require us to provide personal data to Indonesian government authorities.

These authorities may process that information under their own statutory powers, systems and procedures.

5. Other recipients

Where necessary for your matter, we may disclose personal data to professional advisers, translators, technology providers, payment providers and other service providers.

We do not sell your personal data.

6. International data transfers

Some technology, communications or cloud services may process or store data outside Indonesia.

Where personal data is transferred outside Indonesia, we will apply the requirements of applicable Indonesian personal data protection law.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss, destruction or misuse.

8. Retention

We retain personal data for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected and to meet applicable legal, regulatory, accounting and record-keeping requirements.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, update or request deletion of your personal data. You may also have rights concerning consent and certain forms of processing.

We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

10. Website data

Our website may collect limited technical information, including IP address, browser information, device information and information about pages visited.

If we use cookies, analytics or similar technologies, we will process the resulting information in accordance with applicable law and this Privacy Statement.

11. Third-party services

Our website links to government portals and other third-party services. Those services operate independently and may have their own privacy practices.

If you contact us through WhatsApp, email or another third-party communications service, that provider may also process information relating to your communication.

12. Changes

We may update this Privacy Statement when our services, systems or legal obligations change.

The current version will be published on this page.
